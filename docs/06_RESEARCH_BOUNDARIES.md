# Research boundaries

The following statements are intentionally not kernel axioms.

1. Every ECIA structural number has a causal presentation.
2. Causal irreducibles are always equivalent to primitive cycles.
3. Primitive cycles always correspond to arithmetic primes.
4. The causal fraction construction exists in every noncommutative domain.
5. Abstract causal local-global residuals are classical Selmer/Sha without a
   target comparison theorem.
6. Causal iteration proves Mordell--Weil or BSD.
7. A dagger/length realization by itself gives Tomita--Takesaki.
8. Finite determinant identities imply Fredholm determinant identities.
9. Any spectral realization identifies its determinant with the completed
   Riemann zeta.
10. Causal geometry proves RH.
11. Braided/ribbon structure is present in every ordinary concurrency system.
12. A formal \(q\to1\) limit is automatically an \(\mathbf F_1\) geometry.
13. Point-count similarity identifies two arithmetic varieties.
14. The generalized Wilderber restriction calculus is the same object as a
    causal diary; it is a realization of causal observation.
15. Joint conservativity of all ECIA realizations is global; it must be proved
    on an explicitly named domain.
