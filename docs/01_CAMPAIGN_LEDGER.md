## Second closure wave

CA-21 starts by replacing degree-specific calculus with a nonnegative graded
cochain theory, while retaining the existing concrete H1/H2/event modules as
valid lower-degree realizations.  It makes the failures of Phi and Psi to
commute with d, delta and Delta explicit mathematical data and develops their
cohomology/Hodge consequences.

CA-22 does not reopen already-proved q=p^f projective branching.  It targets
the harder remaining source problems: canonical general Ore quotients,
universal properties, two-sided comparison, genuinely nonclassical
factorization and comparison of a general local-field lattice tree with the
abstract q+1 projective tree.

CA-23 cannot close source-side alone.  It requires the real ECIA/GenContinuum
consumer to instantiate the source contracts and publish preservation and
information-loss theorems against exact source/downstream SHAs.

CA-24 introduces additional arithmetic/geometric realizations only through
typed target carriers and explicit comparison theorems.

CA-25 is deliberately downstream of finite Ihara/determinant algebra.  It
requires genuine functional analysis before any Fredholm/global spectral
claim and keeps RH, general BSD and general Langlands endpoints open unless
their missing theorem locks are actually proved.

## Closure contract

A row closes only when its applicable evidence contract is satisfied:

1. mathematical declaration/theorem;
2. positive model;
3. same-type control;
4. same-type failing mutation where independence is claimed;
5. a real consumer when the row is a realization contract;
6. adversarial review of theorem statement and hypotheses;
7. import/proof-hygiene audit;
8. executable exact-SHA build/typecheck evidence;
9. resource/complexity audit where computation is material.

Source presence is therefore weaker than campaign accreditation.
