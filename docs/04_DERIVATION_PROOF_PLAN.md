# Proof plan for ECIA derivation

## A. Environment

Prove CA composition/coherence/dagger/shadow first. Build a correspondence
realization with compositor
\[
R(YX)\cong R(Y)\boxtimes R(X).
\]
ENV02--ENV05 then become transport theorems. ENV10 is witnessed by two
noncommuting causal histories with the same abelian arithmetic shadow.

## B. Structural numbers

Transport causal endodiaries to ECIA endocorrespondences. Prove admissibility
preservation. Prove the prime-profile square and the natural-number inclusion
square. For equal-shadow pairs, explicitly state whether the ECIA realization
separates them or loses the distinction.

## C. Tate

Construct causal fractions and \(v_p\). Only then define
\[
R_T(q)=\chi(q)\widehat{\mathbb Z}.
\]
Prove compatibility of composition with multiplication and scale degree.
Import/bridge the actual adelic/lattice/Pontryagin results only in the ECIA
adapter layer.

## D. Cyclic and Witt

Prove primitive decomposition in finite domains. Build transfer matrices,
trace identities and Euler products. Map primitive cycles to the existing
finite-cycle/Burnside objects. Möbius inversion and F/V comparisons come
after this bridge.

## E. Local-global

Build the abstract diagram
\[
R_G(X)\to\prod_vR_v(X).
\]
Define the compatible local fiber and residual. Then give a separate
comparison theorem for each concrete arithmetic target. Never name the
abstract fiber “Selmer” without that theorem.

## F. Operatorial

Prove arithmetic length and dagger first. A target representation must prove
energy=length and adjoint=dagger. The existing \(n^{it}\) law then follows.
General Tomita infrastructure remains an analytic campaign.

## G. Spectral

Prove formal/finite determinant identities before any global analytic layer.
Require separate evidence for nuclearity, Fredholm, completed zeta and
regularizer. Keep the critical-line implication downstream of all three
locks.

## H. SYS/U

Build compatible realization tuples indexed by one causal source. Define the
loss preorder from factorization between realizations. Only after concrete
families exist attempt joint conservativity; reconstruction is a stronger
later theorem.
