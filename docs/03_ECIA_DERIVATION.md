# Deriving the existing ECIA architecture from causal numbers

## 1. Exact meaning of “derive”

ECIA is not replaced. Its existing constructions become typed realizations of
a prior causal source whenever a theorem proves the bridge.

Every claim is classified:

- **KERNEL**: theorem of causal geometry alone;
- **REALIZATION**: follows after a typed functor/comparison;
- **MODEL**: one concrete ECIA realization of the causal pattern;
- **ANALYTIC**: needs additional analytic infrastructure;
- **OPEN**: no derivation is currently known/proved.

This reproduces ECIA's “cyclic, not globally commutative” foundation.

## 4. Tate, lattices and zero/infinity

For a causal rational
\[

\]
with arithmetic shadow
\[

\]
the Tate realization is
\[
R_T(q)=L_r=r\widehat{\mathbb Z}.
\]

Causal structure supplies:
- composition/multiplication;
- prime valuations;

Target Tate theorems supply:

Thus the causal theory explains the common origin while leaving the genuine

## 5. Burnside--Witt, cycles and divisors

Closure turns an endodiary into cyclic histories. Primitive closed histories,
their powers and counting traces give a common source for:
- cycle decompositions;
- Burnside-like finite cycle objects;
- ghost coordinates;
- Frobenius/Verschiebung as iterate/reindex operations;
- Möbius inversion over primitive/multiple cycles;
- Ihara products in graph-like realizations.

The theorem chain is
\[
X\to Cl(X)\to Prim(X)\to Counts(X)\to Ghost(X).
\]

This does not identify Burnside and Witt targets by definition; it constructs
comparison maps.

## 7. Adeles and local/global systems

A causal source \(X\) may have local realizations
\[
R_v(X)
\]
and one global realization \(R_G(X)\), with
\[
loc:R_G(X)\to\prod_vR_v(X).
\]

The source constructs the compatible local fiber and the globalization
residual class. ECIA adeles package all local views of the same source; SYS
then becomes a theorem that the tuple really has one common causal origin.

This explains why a tuple assembled from unrelated local objects is not a
unification.

## 8. Mordell--Weil, Selmer and Sha

Causal iteration
\[
X\mapsto X^{\circ n}
\]
can be realized as arithmetic multiplication
\[
P\mapsto[n]P.
\]

The abstract local/global fiber has the formal shape of a Selmer condition,
and the residual class has the formal role of failure of globalization.

But
\[
Sel_C(X_E)\cong Sel(E/K,\phi)
\]
and
\[
\mathfrak R_{glob}(X_E)\cong\Sha(E/K)[\phi]
\]
are target comparison theorems, not definitions.

The Néron--Tate height law remains arithmetic-geometric input:
\[
\hat h([n]P)=n^2\hat h(P).
\]

Thus causality derives the source/action/local-global architecture, not the
deep geometry by itself.

## 9. Operatorial/Tomita sector

Arithmetic length gives
\[

\]

A dagger-preserving Hilbert realization gives
\[
\pi(X^\dagger)=\pi(X)^*.

This explains the already-implemented Frobenius--KMS model from one causal
quantity. General FNS weights, Tomita--Takesaki and the continuous W* core

## 10. Ribbon/symmetry sector

Ordinary concurrency only supplies symmetric exchange. A ribbon target needs
a richer causal datum
\[

\]
without imposing involutive symmetry. Therefore G11 is derived from a
**braided causal extension**, not from every diary.

Hecke, Markov, VOA/conformal nets and moonshine remain their own target layers.

## 11. Spectral/zeta sector

\to Z_C
\to Tr(T^n)
\to\det(I-uT)^{-1}

Only after these can the current conditional critical-line implication be
consumed. RH is not a causal-kernel theorem.

## 12. SYS/G13 and strong U

For one causal source \(X\), construct a dependent family
\[
\prod_i^{dep} Enhancement_i(X).
\]

Every branch retains the same source by type. Information loss is the family
of relations
\[
X\sim_iY\iff R_i(X)\simeq R_i(Y).
\]

The strong endpoint is split:

Joint conservativity:
\[
(\forall i,\ R_iX\simeq R_iY)\Rightarrow X\simeq Y.
\]

Reconstruction:
\[
Rec((R_iX)_i)\simeq X.
\]

Neither is assumed globally.
